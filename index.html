Dera Sant Ghar, Harkhowal

Sant Baba Jagjit Singh Ji continues the seva of Dera Sant Ghar through katha of Gurbani and Gurmat vichar, at home in Punjab and with sangats abroad.

The Dera was founded by Sant Jawala Singh Ji, who left the army after serving in France in the First World War and became a disciple of Sant Aiya Singh Ji of Hoti Mardan. In 1918 he settled between Harkhowal and Pandori Bibi, about 11 km southwest of Hoshiarpur, where sangat came for his simple, clear discourses and thousands took Amrit at his hands.
