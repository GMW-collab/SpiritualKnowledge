# Roadmap

Ordered by value for effort. "Effort" is for a developer familiar with the repository.

| # | What | Value | Effort | Depends on |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Learned review of the eleven theme essays**, especially comparative notes and "where views differ" | Essential: turns drafts into trusted teaching material | Reviewer's time; recording it takes minutes on `/review/` | A reviewer chosen by the Dera |
| 2 | **Full YouTube archive** with a free API key | Hundreds of older divans become browsable and themed | 10 minutes (SOURCES.md) | Owner |
| 3 | **Transcripts of Babaji's katha** (YouTube captions via owner OAuth, or speech-to-text of the Gurmatveechar audio with Punjabi review) | The largest single improvement: search inside katha, themes linked by content not just titles, quotable passages with timestamps | Medium to large; Punjabi speech-to-text needs human correction; compute cost to be estimated on a sample before committing | Owner consent; a review process for transcripts |
| 4 | **Source-grounded Q&A** ("What does Babaji say about…") answering only from transcribed katha and Gurbani, citing recordings with timestamps, refusing when nothing relevant is found | High, but only with item 3 in place; without transcripts it would invent | Medium; usage-based model costs | Item 3, editorial policy |
| 5 | **Gurbani lines in more places**: shabad pages for the shabads Babaji's single divans are titled after (Sidh Gosht, Dakhni Oankar, "Paath Pariyo…"), each with its katha | Medium-high: connects 200+ shabad-titled recordings to their Gurbani | Small per shabad | Editor time; lines go through the BaniDB check |
| 6 | **Punjabi interface** (navigation, theme introductions in Gurmukhi) | High for older sangat | Medium (translation and review) | A Punjabi editor |
| 7 | **Guided listening paths** (for example "Japji Sahib in nine evenings", "Introduction to Vichar Sagar") | Medium | Small; built from existing series | Babaji or sevadars choosing the order |
| 8 | **Upload-date accuracy**: the dates are Gurmatveechar's file dates; many older recordings carry a dated title instead | Medium for "newest" listings | Small: prefer dates parsed from titles where present | — |
| 9 | **More curated sources** (other katha of the lineage, Hoti Mardan history) through the feed adapter | Medium | Small per source | Editorial approval of each source |
| 10 | **Saved lists** of recordings on a device | Low-medium | Small (browser storage, no accounts) | — |
| 11 | **Personal reflection journal** | Low; only if kept entirely on the visitor's device | Medium | A privacy review |

Not planned, deliberately: accounts, comments, analytics, a database, or AI-generated pages. Each would add cost, privacy duties or thin content without serving the sangat better.
